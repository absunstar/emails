How Social Temp Mail works

  1. Create or enter an address. Start with a generated temporary address or type the exact inbox you need to check.
  2. Wait for the message. The live inbox uses server-sent events when available and falls back to periodic refresh.
  3. Read only what you need. Copy verification codes, open confirmation links, inspect attachments, or export the stored message when appropriate.
Use temporary email for low-risk, short-term messages. Do not use disposable inboxes for banking, sensitive personal data, long-term password recovery, legal documents, or communications that require durable confidentiality.

Why use a separate temporary inbox?

A temporary address can reduce clutter in your primary mailbox and separate one-time signups or test traffic from long-term accounts. Social Temp Mail also supports multiple saved inboxes in the same browser, unread indicators, message search tools, verification-code detection, attachment downloads, and privacy-oriented message reading.

Temporary email does not make a third-party website trustworthy and does not replace a secure permanent mailbox. Choose the right kind of inbox for the sensitivity and lifetime of the account you are creating.
